# Trap Walker

**One line. One shot. Don't let him die.**

### [Play it now in your browser](https://r4ij3n-droid.github.io/trap-walker/)

![Trap Walker gameplay](screenshot.jpg)

A little guy in a red hoodie is about to walk straight into a pit of spikes, and he is not going to stop. All you've got is your finger, and you only get one line.

Make it count. A bridge over the pit, a hump over the spikes, a ramp up to the ledge, a wall that turns him around, or one long line that does all of it at once. The second you lift your finger, he starts walking. Either your line gets him to the exit, or he doesn't, and you try again.

Use less ink and you earn more stars. Use too little and, well, he falls.

**What's in it**

- 90 handmade levels across 9 worlds: Trap Factory, Rooftops, The Moon, Candy Land, Haunted Castle, Clockwork Tower, Deep Sea, Volcano and Sky Temple.
- Every world brings a new trick: crumbling tiles, springs, low gravity, ghosts you trap under a dome, levers, lasers, ocean currents, lava heat that melts your line and rainbow rings that teleport him.
- Your line doesn't have to be perfect. Wobbly is fine.
- Three stars per level and outfits to unlock.
- Plays in portrait or landscape, in English or Arabic.

Made for YouTube Playables.
